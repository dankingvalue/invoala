# Invoala — 90-Day Content & On-Page Plan

## Why this order

Head term ("free invoice generator") is a 3-6 month battle. Long-tail pages
rank in weeks, bring traffic, and build the domain authority that lifts the
head term. Cheap wins first, authority plays later.

## Phase 1 — Weeks 1-3: long-tail wins (mostly done, keep polishing)

Rank targets you already own:
- /tools/* — invoice-due-date-calculator, late-payment-calculator,
  invoice-tax-calculator, invoice-number-generator. These are low-competition
  queries with real search volume.
- /templates/* — 16 industry templates. Target "[industry] invoice template".
- /learn/* — already strong; interlink tools ↔ learn ↔ templates.

Quick wins to add (each ~1 afternoon, or commissionable):
1. "Invoice template" comparison/roundup INSIDE /invoice-template.
2. "Late payment interest calculator" — you have the calculator; add a
   paragraph-style explainer per country (US/UK/EU/AU) on separate URLs:
   `late-payment-interest-us`, `-uk`, `-eu`. Each is a rankable page.
3. Free downloads: "Invoice template PDF" pages that render a printable
   blank invoice (built on the same generator — instant content + capture).

## Phase 2 — Weeks 2-6: linkable assets

1. Publish the payment data you already have at
   /research/invoice-payment-report and add fresh charts ("median time to
   payment by industry", "share of freelancers who invoice late").
2. Add a free "Invoice PDF generator" embed page (you have /embed) and
   offer it to freelancer blogs — embedded widget = backlink + traffic.

## Phase 3 — Weeks 4-12: authority content

One strong post per week, 1,200+ words, original angle (not another
"how to write an invoice"):
1. "We analyzed 1,000 freelancer invoices: what makes clients pay faster"
   (data-driven, from your research page).
2. "Invoice late-payment laws by country (US/UK/EU/AU) — what you can
   actually charge."
3. "Free invoice tools compared: the no-sign-up test" — test Wave, Zoho,
   invoice-generator.com, Invoala with the same invoice, screenshot results.
   (Links out to competitors = link-worthy for a comparison post.)
4. "How to invoice clients as a [designer/developer/photographer/contractor]"
   series targeting each of your 16 industry templates.

## On-page hygiene checklist (already done, verify monthly)

- [x] One canonical URL per keyword (www host) — fixed & deployed
- [x] No duplicate tool pages (301 /free-invoice-generator) — fixed
- [x] Titles under ~65 chars, brand once — fixed
- [x] FAQ schema on generator pages
- [ ] Re-check canonical + indexation in GSC once you verify the site

## What NOT to do

- No doorway pages ("free invoice" x 20 keyword variants — Google will
  penalize; one URL per intent).
- No paid guest post farms / PBNs. Link velocity is too fast for a 1-week
  domain and the risk is deindexation.
- Don't move /invoice-generator — it's the consolidated target now.
