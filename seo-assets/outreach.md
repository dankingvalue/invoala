# Invoala — Backlink Outreach Templates

Cold outreach is a numbers game: 30-60 personalized emails per week, 5-10%
reply rate is normal. Track responses in a spreadsheet.

## Targets (start here)

- Freelancer/creator resource roundups ("best tools for freelancers",
  "invoicing apps for designers/developers") — search:
  `"best invoice" freelancers roundup`, `"tools I use" freelance designer`,
  `inurl:resources invoicing tools`
- "Best free tools" listicles — `"free tools" for small business`,
  `"invoice software" comparison`
- Accounting/finance-for-freelancers blogs (they write about invoicing).
- Indie/SaaS founder directories (many are free self-serve, see listings.md).

## Where to promote without a backlink (still valuable)

- Reddit: r/freelance, r/smallbusiness, r/Entrepreneur, r/copywriting —
  answer questions with substance, mention the tool once and only when
  relevant. Never spam-link.
- IndieHackers, X/Twitter "build in public": weekly screenshots of
  the generator + metrics.
- TikTok/Shorts/Reels: 15-30s screen recordings — "how I invoice clients
  in 10 seconds" + "watch me make an invoice on my phone".
